Added

- **`mnema audit antipatterns` reads what agents accepted.** Three signals, a reading and never a pause: the rules a person
  accepted (or that carry an address) which an agent then took out of force, the chains of three decisions or more (A to B
  to C), and the agent accepts whose note quotes no words of the person (typed or an option chosen). Who accepted is the
  `which` on the event, and none declared is not read as a person. They are in the human page and, under `agentAccepts`, in
  `--json`; the agent's `audit_antipatterns` tool is unchanged.
