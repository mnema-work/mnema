Changed

- **The terminal carries no colour of the brand, only weight.** The console's prompt and the mark on a picked row are bold, the rules of the input area are drawn in the terminal's own foreground, and the highlighted word of the list of words has neither colour nor weight. The list of words stays faint as a whole, the chosen row included: its marker is bold and the rest of the row, the word and its description, is as faint as the rows beside it. The magenta accent is gone from every role of the renderer and from the layout. The three severities (red, green and yellow, as before) are the only hues, and the greys under a sent command and `(proposed)` yellow and `(accepted)` green are unchanged. A field an actor wrote that holds its own `ESC[35m` is no longer a sequence this product writes, so the port that lets the product's own sequences through now escapes it like any other control byte.
- **The console opens on a new five-row banner**, in full blocks, 52 columns wide, drawn in bold. The shortest window it is drawn on falls from 80 by 42 to 80 by 39, worked out from the drawing as before. The README says the number the code has.
- **`mnema status` folds three lines that were raw strings.** The paragraph under "Not in git yet", the one under "Kept aside by a pull" and the line that says how many of the rules in force an agent accepted now go through the renderer that folds, so a terminal no longer cuts them in the middle of a word. Piped, the output is byte for byte what it was.
- **Every recording on the front page is a pair, drawn on GitHub's dark and light page colours.** `recordings/<name>-dark.gif` is drawn on `#0d1117` and `recordings/<name>-light.gif` on `#ffffff`, with GitHub's own terminal palette for each (red, green and yellow keep 4.5:1 or better on both, in their full colours; faint text is the theme's own faint, whatever contrast that gives), and the README shows the one that sits on the reader's page. `.github/the-recordings/render.mjs` is the one place the theme is written; the release preparation calls it for the console recording. The four command-line recordings fold every line the binary prints at a space, so none is cut in the middle of a word, and they are made again from this version's binary.
