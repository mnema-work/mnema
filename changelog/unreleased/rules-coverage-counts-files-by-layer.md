- **`mnema rules --coverage` counts how many of the project's files fall under each layer of the rules, with no score.** Run with no path, it walks the files once (the walk `mnema link` uses to say what an address covers) and prints, in all and per top-level folder, how many are refused by a rule that refuses a write, how many ask for a person, how many are governed, and how many are under no rule. Each file counts once, under the strongest layer over it. It prints numbers and no percentage; it names the folders left out as not hand-written, and past the walk ceiling of 50,000 files it says every count is a floor. `--json` gives the same.
