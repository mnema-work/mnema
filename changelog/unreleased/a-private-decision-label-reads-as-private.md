Changed

- **A private or global decision's label is read with its tree, and a cut search says how to reach the rest.** Each tree numbers its own decisions, so a private `ADR-78` and the public `ADR-78` read the same. Where a decision is shown to a person (`mnema status`, in force and awaiting a judgement; `show`, including the decisions it replaced or was replaced by, and the same names on a `search` hit; the heading of `mnema decision review`) the label of one held outside the public tree now ends in its tree, `ADR-78 (private)` or `ADR-78 (global)`. The public label is unchanged, nothing stored or signed changes, a label is accepted as before, and the line a `decision` write or move prints keeps its shape, `Recorded decision ADR-78 (<id>)`. In the JSON of `bootstrap` and of the decisions a record names, one outside the public tree carries `scope`, and a public one carries none. When `mnema search` finds more records than it shows, a line under the header says how many matched and that the rest is reached by a window of dates (`--from` and `--to`); there is no offset.
