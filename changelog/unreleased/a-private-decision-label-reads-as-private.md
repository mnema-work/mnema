Changed

- **A private or global decision's label is read with its tree, and a cut search says how to reach the rest.** Each tree numbers its own decisions, so a private `ADR-78` and the public `ADR-78` read the same; wherever a decision is shown (`status`, `show`, `decision review`, the line a `decision` write or move prints, and the `bootstrap` and `read_record` replies of the MCP) the label of one held outside the public tree now ends in its tree, `ADR-78 (private)` or `ADR-78 (global)`. The public label is unchanged, nothing stored or signed changes, and a label is accepted as before. In the JSON of `bootstrap` and of a decision's other end, a decision outside the public tree carries `scope`; a public one carries none. When `mnema search` finds more records than it shows, a line under the header says how many matched and to reach the rest with a window of dates (`--from` and `--to`); there is no offset.
